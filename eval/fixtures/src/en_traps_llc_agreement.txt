AMENDED AND RESTATED LIMITED LIABILITY COMPANY AGREEMENT
OF
[[CompanyName|NORTHWIND HARBOR HOLDINGS LLC]]

ARTICLE I
FORMATION; NAME; PURPOSE

Section 1.1 Formation. The Company was formed as a Delaware limited liability company under the Delaware Limited Liability Company Act, 6 Del. C. § 18-101 et seq. (the "Act"), by the filing of a Certificate of Formation with the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware.

Section 1.2 Name. The name of the Company is "[[CompanyName|Northwind Harbor Holdings LLC]]".

Section 1.3 Purpose. The purpose of the Company is to engage in any lawful act or activity for which limited liability companies may be formed under the Act.

ARTICLE II
DEFINITIONS

"Affiliate" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly Controls, is Controlled by or is under common Control with such Person.

"Board of Managers" means the board of managers of the Company described in Article V.

"Capital Contribution" means the total amount of cash and the agreed value of property contributed to the Company by a Member.

"Change of Control" has the meaning set forth in Section 9.4.

"Fair Market Value" means the price a willing buyer would pay a willing seller, as determined by the Independent Appraiser.

"Force Majeure Event" means any act of God, flood, fire, epidemic, Governmental Authority order, war or terrorism.

"Internal Revenue Code" or "Code" means the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended.

"Preferred Return" means an amount equal to eight percent (8%) per annum, compounded annually.

"Tag-Along Notice" has the meaning set forth in Section 10.3(b).

ARTICLE III
MEMBERS; UNITS

Section 3.1 Members. The names, addresses and Units of the Members are set forth on Schedule A, as amended from time to time by the Board of Managers.

Section 3.2 Units. As of the Effective Date, the Company has issued 10,000 Class A Units and 2,500 Class B Units.

ARTICLE IV
ALLOCATIONS AND DISTRIBUTIONS

Section 4.1 Allocations. Profits and Losses shall be allocated in accordance with Treasury Regulations Section 1.704-1(b)(2)(iv) and Section 704(c) of the Code.

Section 4.2 Tax Distributions. The Company shall make Tax Distributions no later than ninety (90) days after the end of each Fiscal Year, beginning with Fiscal Year 2025.

ARTICLE V
MANAGEMENT

Section 5.1 Board of Managers. The Board of Managers shall consist of three (3) Managers. The initial Managers are [[PersonName|Constance Varga-Ellison]], [[PersonName|Rupert Ashdown]] and [[PersonName|Imelda Szabo]].

Section 5.2 Standard of Care. Consistent with Gantler v. Stephens, 965 A.2d 695 (Del. 2009), and In re Walt Disney Co. Derivative Litigation, 906 A.2d 27 (Del. 2006), the Managers owe the duties of care and loyalty.

ARTICLE VI
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Section 6.1 Notices. Notices shall be in writing and delivered to the Company's registered agent, the [[?CompanyName|Corporation Trust Company]], [[?Address|1209 Orange Street, Wilmington, Delaware 19801]].

Section 6.2 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles. Sections 2.1 through 2.4 of the Uniform Commercial Code shall not apply.

Section 6.3 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by DocuSign or other Electronic Signature, each of which shall be deemed an original.

Section 6.4 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, together with the Exhibits and Schedules hereto, constitutes the Entire Agreement among the Members and supersedes the Original Agreement dated as of [[Date|March 1, 2019]].

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the undersigned have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.
